01 — The Problem

What Is Paint Correction?

Every car's clear coat picks up damage over time — wash swirls, spider webbing, bird bath etching, oxidation, and light scratches that dull the finish and hide the true depth of your paint. On dark paint, especially black, every one of those defects shows up under direct light.

Paint correction is the process of machine-polishing away a microscopic layer of clear coat to level out that damage, revealing a deep, glass-like reflection underneath. It's the difference between a car that's "clean" and a car that looks brand new.

  • Removes swirl marks, wash-induced scratches & holograms
  • Eliminates oxidation and restores true paint color and depth
  • Maximizes gloss and reflection before sealing or coating

06 — FAQ

Paint Correction FAQ

Will paint correction remove every scratch?

Correction removes anything within the clear coat — swirls, most light-to-moderate scratches, oxidation, and water spots. Deep scratches that go through the clear coat into the base color can be improved but not fully erased without paint touch-up.

Is machine polishing safe for my clear coat?

Yes. We measure clear coat thickness before correcting and use the least aggressive pad/compound combination needed for your paint's condition, so we only remove the microscopic amount necessary.

How long will the results last?

The correction itself is permanent until new swirls occur from washing. We recommend sealing it with a ceramic coating so the glossy, corrected finish stays protected and easier to maintain for years.

How do I know which stage my car needs?

Every booking starts with a free paint inspection under direct light. We'll show you the defects and recommend the lightest stage that gets the results you want — you approve the plan and price before we start.

Do you come to me?

Yes, we're a fully mobile unit. We bring all water, power, and equipment needed and perform the correction right in your driveway or lot.
